## Pass bar for a knowledge base

A knowledge base passes when, over the golden set:

- average correctness is at least 1.7 of 2 (auto proxy: key-point coverage >= 0.85),
- no single answer scores 0 (auto proxy: no question below 0.5 coverage),
- at least 90% of answers are grounded,
- zero invalid citations, and
- zero confidently wrong answers.

## How the auto grader relates to this rubric

`evals/run_golden.py grade` checks key-point phrases, grounding and citation validity
mechanically. It cannot detect wrong statements or jargon. Use `run_golden.py judge` to
produce one judge prompt per question and run them through a model (or a human) for the
correctness, plain-language and confidently-wrong checks.
